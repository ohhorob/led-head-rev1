# Dedicated 5 V power module requirements

The current design is implemented in power-manager.kicad_sch and documented in README.md.

- One right-angle XT60 input from the dedicated, externally regulated 5 V supply.
- Direct 5 V distribution to the carrier's existing ten-pin Mini-Fit connector.
- Peripheral 3.3 V generated by Rob's WEBENCH TPS62091 design, retaining its 2 A target, 1 µH inductor, 22 µF input/output, 10 nF charge-pump capacitor, 3.9 nF soft-start capacitor and 499k PG pull-up.
- Package capacitors drawn connected to the actual pins they serve.
- Practical passive packages for hand/hot-air assembly: 0805 and 1206. Retain the explicitly selected TPS62091 QFN.
- Simple green power indicator and 2.54 mm signal/GND probe pairs.
- User places components, draws outline and routes the board.
- Battery power is a separate future interchangeable module. This board has no battery module, source selection, reverse-blocking controller, ADC divider, standby LDO or telemetry harness.

The abandoned combined design is archived in ../.history/power-manager-combined-before-dedicated-5v-20261121.zip. It is not the basis for further layout.
